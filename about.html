African Express

The Heart of Africa, Served in Minutes.

African Express started with a simple mission: to make the rich culinary map of Africa accessible to everyone, everywhere.

We grew tired of choosing between healthy, authentic meals and the convenience of quick service. So, we combined the two. Our kitchen uses traditional recipes passed down through generations reimagined for the modern palate Every dish is a tribute to the diverse cultures and bold seasonings that make African cuisine world-class. From the smoky depth of our Jollof rice to the fiery kick of our grilled Suya, we bring you the authentic taste of the continent without the wait
